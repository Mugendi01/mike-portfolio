01 Healthcare Technology / Web Application Role-Based Health Care Management System Structured healthcare operations with access designed around each user's responsibility.

The Problem

Small healthcare facilities can depend on fragmented or manual processes for patient information and daily clinical operations. That makes records harder to organise, responsibilities harder to separate, and sensitive information easier to expose than it should be.

The Solution

I designed and developed a role-based management system for small healthcare facilities. Instead of giving every user the same access, the architecture maps system functions to the responsibilities of administrators, doctors, nurses, and receptionists.

My Role

I was responsible for the system architecture, database design, access control, backend development, frontend implementation, feature development, testing, and troubleshooting. The project was designed with responsible handling of sensitive patient information in mind, including principles associated with the Kenya Data Protection Act. It is not presented as legally certified.

Key Features

  • Secure authentication and role-based access control
  • Patient registration and record management
  • Doctor, nurse, receptionist, and administrator workflows
  • Dashboards with relevant functions for each role
  • Structured healthcare information and controlled access to sensitive data

Tech Stack

The Impact

The project demonstrates how I translate a real operational problem into a structured system while balancing workflows, permissions, data, and security.

03 IT Asset Management / Internal Business System ICT Asset Management System (ICT AMS) A digital chain of custody for equipment that needs to be issued, found, returned, and reported on.

The Problem

Projectors and video-conferencing equipment could move between staff and teams without a reliable view of who had an item, when it was due back, where it had moved, or whether it was available, overdue, damaged, or under repair. Manual movement books and fragmented tracking made accurate reporting difficult.

The Solution

I developed ICT AMS to centralise equipment registration, issuing, returning, booking, movement tracking, condition records, repairs, and reporting. It creates a structured digital workflow for accountability instead of relying on disconnected manual records.

My Role

I handled requirements analysis, system and database design, backend and frontend development, equipment workflows, reporting functionality, testing, troubleshooting, and system improvement.

Key Features

  • Authentication with admin and user roles
  • Equipment registration, issue forms, booking, and availability tracking
  • Expected and actual return dates with overdue tracking and alerts
  • Condition, repair, vendor, and equipment movement records
  • Search, calendar management, email notifications where implemented
  • Weekly, monthly, and fortnightly overdue reports with PDF generation

Tech Stack

PHPMySQLXAMPPHTMLCSSJavaScript

Database: ict_equipment_tracker

The Impact

ICT AMS improves equipment accountability, visibility, availability management, record keeping, and reporting while reducing dependence on manual tracking.
